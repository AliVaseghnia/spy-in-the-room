                The short version

                How to play

                One phone. One secret. Spies hiding in plain sight.

                1. 01

                  Get the room ready

                  Add 4–12 names. Pick a location, or bring your own secret.

                2. 02

                  Reveal, hide & pass

                  The named player reveals their card, then taps once to hide it and pass the phone to the next player. The final hide starts the round.

                3. 03

                  Ask, bluff, accuse

                  Talk around the location without giving it away. Answer in character if you like; don’t say your role outright. Watch who sounds lost.

                4. 04

                  Vote, then guess

                  Pick the spy. If you’re right, the spy gets one last shot at the secret.

                Players
                4–12
                Round
                3, 5, or 8 min
                Spies
                1 or 2

                Win condition

                Find the spies before the clock runs out. Pick the wrong player and the spies take the round.
